Sh. Mohan Lal Bloch

Founder & Visionary

Our Legacy

A Dream Carried From Patiala

Sh. Mohan Lal Bloch arrived in the city of Chandigarh with nothing but the clothes on his back and a fire in his chest that no hardship could extinguish. He had left Patiala, Punjab as a young man — no savings, no connections, no safety net — carrying only an unshakeable belief that honest work and patient hands could build something the world would one day notice. There were days he went without a full meal. There were nights he spent sleepless. But he never once considered turning back. Punjab had given him his roots. Now he was going to give his family their wings.

He opened his first furniture shop with borrowed tools and borrowed time, working from before sunrise until long after the city had gone to sleep. He learned the grain of every wood, the temperament of every joint, the way teak weathers and sheesham deepens with age. He built each piece with his own hands, and every customer who walked out of that shop carried not just furniture but something made with genuine love and craft. Word spread slowly, the way true reputation always does — quietly, steadily, and without any shortcuts. Through storms, through floods, through years when business was lean and doubt crept close, Sh. Mohan Lal Bloch simply worked harder. That was his answer to everything life threw at him. Work harder, care more, and never compromise on what you put your name to.

One shop became two. Two became five. Each new location was not merely an expansion but a testament to fifty years of relentless devotion to a single craft. He never chased trends or cut corners to chase profit. He built furniture the way he had built his life — slowly, deliberately, and with the intention that it should last. Customers who bought from him in the early years came back decades later with their children, then their grandchildren, asking for pieces they knew would outlive the occasions they were bought for. In a world full of the temporary and the disposable, Sh. Mohan Lal Bloch made things that endured. That, more than any award or recognition, was the measure of the empire he built.

In 2021, he left this world. And the grief that followed was not the kind that fades with time — it is the kind that lives permanently in the chest, surfacing without warning in the middle of ordinary days. His family misses him every single morning. There are tears that still come without asking, at the dinner table, in the workshop, in the quiet moments when a decision needs to be made and everyone instinctively turns toward the chair that is no longer filled. His sons carry his photograph close. His grandsons grew up hearing stories of the man whose name they are now entrusted to honour. But it is not only his family who feels the weight of his absence. The workers who stood beside him from the very beginning — men who spent decades measuring wood and finishing surfaces under his watchful, encouraging eye — grieve him with an equal and profound tenderness. They remember exactly how he spoke to them, how he never once made a worker feel small, how he would bring tea to the workshop floor himself and sit among them as though the distance between owner and craftsman simply did not exist in his world. His loss has left an open wound that no time, no success, and no achievement can fully close. Some people leave a gap when they go. He left a canyon.

And yet the family carries on — not because the grief has lessened, but because he taught them by example that carrying on is itself the highest form of love. His two sons and two grandsons now steward the legacy forward, each one shaped by the same values he lived by without ever preaching them: integrity in every transaction, craft in every piece, and the humility to remember where it all began — a young man from Patiala with empty pockets and an enormous heart. Today, Sunny Furniture is more than a business. It is a living tribute to one extraordinary life. Every sofa crafted, every dining table delivered, every family that gathers around a piece bearing our name — all of it is woven from the same thread that Sh. Mohan Lal Bloch pulled tight with his own hands five decades ago. We do not simply sell furniture. We carry forward a promise made by a man who proved that dreams, when held tightly enough and worked for honestly enough, always find their way home.
